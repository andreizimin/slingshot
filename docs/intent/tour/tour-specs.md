# Getting-Started Tour — EARS Specs

Design: `tour-design.md` · Prefix: `TOUR`

- [x] **TOUR-001**: The tour shall consist of eight steps in this order (ship, planet, speed, plan, try, fly, swing, result), each with a title and caption; the first shall focus a close-up on the ship and say it is heading for a planet without enough fuel to reach its destination; "Your speed" shall explain both the speed number and the ship's two speed arrows; "Fuel, and planning is free" shall explain fuel and planning while paused; the result caption shall say "Boost!" and that passing behind sped the ship up, or "Brake!" and that passing in front slowed it down (this side slows you down), stating the change, and offer I'm ready and Try the other side; when the change is under 0.05 km/s it shall offer Try again.
- [x] **TOUR-002**: When the start screen's Tutorial is chosen, or the ? ("How to play") button is pressed, the cockpit shall reset the flight to the tutorial scenario and start the tutorial from step 1.
- [x] **TOUR-003**: Steps whose rule is "tap" shall advance on Next (button, Enter or Space); "Your turn" shall offer Next once there is a planned sideways change in either direction and the prediction ends in a departure; "Fly!" shall advance by itself when play starts; the result step shall end the tour on I'm ready.
- [x] **TOUR-004**: While the tour is active, the cockpit shall show a caption card with the step count, Skip tour, title, caption and the step's button, docked in the card strip (`TOUR-016`), only while paused, except the result step, which shall show its card when the tour flight ends (departed or crashed), and, if paused mid-flight, a "Paused" card saying how to carry on; the normal centred messages shall not show while the tour is active.
- [x] **TOUR-005**: If the tour flight crashes, then the result card shall say so and offer Try again, which shall reset the flight and return to "Your turn" with a note to swing a little wider.
- [x] **TOUR-006**: While the tour is on steps 1 to 3 (ship, planet, speed), the cockpit shall ignore steering and play inputs; on "Fuel, and planning is free" and "Your turn" it shall accept steering but not play; on "Fly!" it shall accept play; R shall act only once a result card is shown: after a crash it shall do the same as Try again, after a departure with a change over 0.05 km/s either way it shall do the same as I'm ready, otherwise the same as Try again; mid-flight it shall do nothing. Pause shall always be accepted.
- [x] **TOUR-007**: During step 1, the camera shall centre on the ship at 300 km/px; on every other step it shall centre on the planet at the normal zoom, easing between them; in both cases the centre and zoom shall be those of the play area (`COCKPIT-CAM-007`).
- [x] **TOUR-008**: While a card is shown, the cockpit shall highlight its target: a pulsing yellow outline on HUD targets (speed readout, fuel bar, time buttons) and a pulsing dashed yellow ring on world targets (ship, planet, the ship's speed arrows).
- [x] **TOUR-009**: During steps 1 and 2, the cockpit shall draw a story-only "Destination: far away" marker at the right edge; it shall have no effect on the game.
- [x] **TOUR-010**: The tour shall send `slingshot_tour_start` (`auto`), `slingshot_tour_complete` (`change`) and `slingshot_tour_skip` (`step`), counts only.
- [x] **TOUR-011**: During the tour flight, on a close pass (closest approach under 2 million km), when the ship has just passed its closest point to the planet, the cockpit shall pause once and show "How the slingshot works", explaining that the planet's gravity bends the path and, because the planet is moving, drags the ship along as it swings behind; Keep flying shall resume play at the previous speed. If the flight ends before the checkpoint, the tour shall go straight to the result card.
- [x] **TOUR-012**: While a tour card is shown, → shall do the same as the card's button and ← and a Back button on the card shall return one step; going back shall be possible on steps 2 to 6 ("Fly!") and not on the mid-flight checkpoint or the result. → and ← shall never steer the ship.
- [x] **TOUR-013**: When the player presses I'm ready or Skip tour, the cockpit shall end the tutorial and start the level (see `LEVEL-010`).
- [x] **TOUR-014**: The tutorial shall teach both sides: "Your turn" shall offer passing behind (drag down, speeds you up) or in front (drag up, slows you down); the side flown shall be recorded at launch, and the mid-swing checkpoint shall explain that side (behind: the pull drags you along; in front: the pull tugs you backwards, so this side slows you down). Try the other side shall reset the flight to "Your turn" with a "Now try the other side" prompt.
- [x] **TOUR-015**: When the page loads, the cockpit shall show a start screen over the paused tutorial scenario, with the title "Slingshot", one line saying the player borrows speed from planets to reach places their fuel cannot, and two buttons, Tutorial and Sample mission; Tutorial shall start the tutorial (`TOUR-002`) and Sample mission shall start the level (`LEVEL-010`); while the start screen is shown, steering, play and R shall do nothing, and Enter shall choose Tutorial.
- [x] **TOUR-016**: The tour card shall dock in the card strip: horizontally centred, at most 640 px wide and at least 16 px from each side, with its bottom edge 16 px above the top of the time buttons (68 px on screens narrower than 600 px, clearing the feedback button there); it shall never be positioned by its target.
